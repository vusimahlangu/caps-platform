Chapter 1: Algebra

1. Introduction

Mastering **quadratic equations** and the **Nature of Roots** (\(\Delta\)).

2. Rules & Formulae

The Quadratic Formula:

\[ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \]

Nature of Roots depends on the Discriminant \(\Delta = b^2 - 4ac\).

3. 4 Worked Examples

  1. Solve \(x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0 \implies (x-2)(x-3)=0\).
  2. Solve \(2x^2 + x - 5 = 0\) (to 2 decimals).
  3. Inequality: \(x^2 - 9 \geq 0 \implies x \leq -3\) or \(x \geq 3\).
  4. Surds: \(\sqrt{x+2} = x\).

4. Illustration: The Nature of Roots

5. Chapter Exercises

  1. Solve for \(x\): \((2x - 1)(x + 4) = 0\).
  2. Show that the roots of \(x^2 + kx + 9 = 0\) are equal if \(k = \pm 6\).

6. Summary

The discriminant \(\Delta\) is the DNA of an equation.
